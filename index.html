APPLICATION STAGES & STATUS GUIDE

WHOSE TURN CHIPS
ACTION NEEDED You have an active task to complete (e.g. choose an interview time slot, complete an assessment, upload a transcript).
WAITING FOR EMPLOYER Our team is reviewing your materials or scheduling the next step. You are all set and do not need to do anything.
CLOSED The application has ended (e.g. position filled or candidate not selected). This never affects your other active applications.
HIRING STAGES
SUBMITTED You sent your application and our system verified receipt.
RECEIVED We have your application and logged your profile in our applicant tracking system.
SCREENING Recruiter is reviewing your background, resume, and portfolio against role criteria.
ASSESSMENT Online technical challenge, SQL/coding test, or portfolio review (for specific roles).
INTERVIEW Meet the team on video call for technical, design, and culture conversations.
AWAITING DECISION The hiring panel debriefs and consolidates evaluations to decide on offer extension.
OFFER Formal offer package prepared; your review, questions, or acceptance requested.
CLOSED OUTCOMES "Not selected", "Withdrawn", or "Offer accepted". Each application is judged independently.
